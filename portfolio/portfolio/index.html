01 — About

Someone who'd rather ship a working model than talk about one.

I'm a B.Tech Computer Science student specializing in AI & ML at Graphic Era University, currently in my second year. Most of what I know, I learned by breaking something first — a gesture-recognition pipeline that misfired on every third frame, a model that overfit until I actually understood why.

I care about the gap between a notebook that works and a system someone can actually rely on. That means writing tests, structuring code so the next person (often future me) can read it, and treating "it works on my machine" as a starting point, not a finish line.

Right now I'm deepening my machine learning fundamentals, working through computer vision problems, and getting comfortable with AWS — while looking for an internship where I can trade textbook problems for real ones.
